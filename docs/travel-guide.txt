GRIMDARK TRAVEL GUIDE

Start the game with your usual Grimdark launcher.

GET TO BURIAL HILL
1. Once in the world, press F1.
2. Choose Travel, then Active quest destinations.
3. Choose Burial Hill and press Enter. Walking starts automatically.
4. At the arrival announcement, press Control+M. Choose Burial Hill Entrance and press Backspace to walk toward it.
5. If enemies hurt you, travel stops. Handle the fight, then press Control+apostrophe to retry your destination.
6. At the cave, N selects nearby neutral objects and entrances. Select Cave Entrance and press J to enter.

WALK TO A TRACKER TARGET
Use the existing tracker to select an enemy, NPC, item or entrance, then press Control+semicolon.
The word "distant" does not prevent walking. The target still needs a connected route loaded by the game.
Moving enemies are followed until you are close enough to take over. Press J to attack or interact.
The helper does not attack or select another enemy automatically.

RETURN TO TOWN
Press Control+Shift+L. Wait for the destination list, choose Devil's Crossing, and press Enter.
This uses your personal rift and the game's unlocked riftgates. It cannot bypass areas that prohibit rifts.

STOP OR RESUME
F8 stops. W, A, S or D also stops and gives you manual movement.
Control+apostrophe retries your selected map or quest destination.
Control+semicolon starts walking to your current tracker target, or stops if travel is already running.
Control+Shift+semicolon opens the Travel menu directly.
Pausing, changing focus, opening another screen, harmful ground or a blocked route stops travel.
Travel never resumes by itself.

LIMITS
Only your existing tracker, map markers and places named by active quests are offered.
The helper does not reveal unexplored enemies or automatically enter dungeons.
If a route is blocked, select and interact with a nearby door or entrance, then retry.
Regional progress announcements currently give distance to the next route point, not the whole journey.
